# Brand notes: "Slow Miles" (fictional demo creator)

This file is invented for the demo. It does not describe a real person or business.

The creator runs an Instagram blog called Slow Miles about running for beginners
over 35. Most followers are office workers who have never run more than 5 km and
are afraid of knee injuries. The main pain points the audience mentions in
comments: no time after work, shame about being slow, and getting bored after
two weeks.

Tone of voice: calm, friendly, a bit self-ironic, no "no pain no gain" slogans.
The creator never shames slow runners and often says "slow is still faster than
the couch".

Content pillars:
1. Run-walk plans for complete beginners.
2. Injury prevention: warm-ups, shoes, strength for knees.
3. Mindset and habits: how to keep running when motivation is gone.
4. Honest stories from the creator's own first marathon (finished in 5h 12m).

Products: a free 3-week "Couch to 3K" checklist as a lead magnet, and a paid
6-week online program "First 5K" with weekly group calls. Price of the program
is 49 EUR. Goal for this quarter: 300 sign-ups to the free checklist and 40
sales of the program.

Content plan: 3 reels a week (one plan, one mistake breakdown, one story), one
carousel with a weekly plan every Monday, stories with Q&A on Thursdays.
Competitors are big running brands with elite-athlete content; Slow Miles wins by
being relatable to people who feel too old or too slow to start.
